Endure in Faith Publisher

Endure in Faith Publisher is a private publishing tool used only by the Endure in Faith ministry team (Joshua Wilborn) to schedule and post its own short videos to its own TikTok account, @endureinfaithaha.

The team films and edits all of its content itself. The tool lets the account owner sign in with TikTok (Login Kit), then upload a finished video and its caption to that same account through the Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other TikTok user’s account or data.
