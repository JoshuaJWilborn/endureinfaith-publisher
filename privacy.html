Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Endure in Faith Publisher (“the Tool”) is operated by Joshua Wilborn for the Endure in Faith ministry. It is used only by its owner to publish the owner’s own videos to the owner’s own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account via video.upload and video.publish.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

How it is used

Data is used only to upload and publish the owner’s videos and confirm their status. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The Tool sets no tracking cookies and shows no ads.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the owner’s private server, never in the browser, and are only accessible to the owner.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the Tool is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps), after which stored tokens are deleted.
